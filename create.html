Preserving Our Heritage

Every story you share becomes part of South Dallas's living history. Like the historic Forest Theater shown here, our community has countless stories of resilience, joy, and cultural significance that deserve to be remembered.

The Forest Theater was more than just a cinema. It was a gathering place, a community hub, and a symbol of Black excellence in Dallas. Your stories carry that same weight and importance.

By contributing to CEDARS, you're ensuring that future generations understand where we came from and the richness of our shared heritage.
