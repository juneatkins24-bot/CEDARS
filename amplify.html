Sharing Guidelines

When amplifying South Dallas stories, please keep these guidelines in mind:

  • Always credit storytellers and community members featured in content
  • Use official CEDARS hashtags to connect with the broader community
  • Tag @cedars_southdallas in your posts so we can share and celebrate
  • Respect the privacy and dignity of all community members
  • Share accurate information about locations and historical context
  • Encourage others to visit South Dallas and experience stories in person
  • Celebrate the diversity and richness of South Dallas culture
